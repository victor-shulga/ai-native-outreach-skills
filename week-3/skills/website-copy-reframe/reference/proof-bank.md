# Proof bank: inventory before writing

Step 2, shared by all modes. Every claim on the site must trace back to a real, sourced proof
point. Build this table first; writing then becomes assembly instead of invention. Flag anything
without a source and do not ship it. Tag each proof point with the offer or vertical it supports,
so service pages pull the right one.

## Trust inventory
Legal entity and registration · certifications and memberships · geographic coverage · years in
business · team make-up (qualifications) · working hours and time zone overlap · security (NDA,
secure file transfer) · IP terms.

## Rules
- **Source it or cut it.** No unsourced benchmarks. Write "all figures from delivered projects"
  only if that is true.
- **One case per claim per page.** Do not reuse the same case on every offer page; match it.
- **Heritage stays heritage.** A legacy business line or a founder's earlier company can support
  domain credibility. It is never presented as a service on offer today.
- Keep the bank inside the copy deck so the company can extend it as new proof arrives.
