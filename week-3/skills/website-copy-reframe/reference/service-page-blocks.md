# Service and offer page blocks

Mode `service-page`. The job differs from the homepage: ONE service in depth, bottom of funnel,
built to rank. The homepage picks one champion across the company; a service page goes deep on one
offer for a buyer already looking for that service. Reuse the canvas, narrowed to this offer.

Give each block a rationale line naming its canvas cell. Follow the company's voice rules.

## Block order

### 1. Hero
```
[Service name, as the market searches for it]
H1: <the champion outcome this service delivers> + <one proof metric>
Sub: <one line: how it works, what makes it real>
CTA: <the demo / sample next step>
Trust: <2-3 numbers relevant to THIS service>
```
*Rationale:* H1 = capability + benefit + a number from the proof bank. A search-friendly service
name doubles as the SEO H1.

### 2. What it is / what gets delivered (question 3)
Name the concrete deliverables: formats, standards, tolerances. The actual output, not
"solutions".
> Example for a geospatial service: "Classified LAS/LAZ point clouds, ground and off-ground
> separation, powerline extraction, DEM/DTM/DSM, contour generation."

### 3. Problems it fixes (limitations → problems, scoped to this service)
Three or four bullets: the specific pain this service removes for the champion, taken from the
left half of the canvas and filtered to this offer.

### 4. Benefits (in numbers where possible)
The outcomes of using it, tied to the champion's work. Lead with a number from the proof bank.

### 5. Proof (cases and testimonial matched to this offer)
The one or two cases tagged to THIS offer in the proof bank, result first, plus one attributed
testimonial. Never borrow another offer's case.

### 6. How it works / engagement (questions 2 and 4)
The steps and the commercial model: turnaround, per-deliverable or dedicated team, start time,
security and NDA. This answers "when would I use it".

### 7. FAQ / objections
Answer the buying blockers for this offer (from the ICP's known objections): IP and NDA, QA and
standards, data security and residency, price, "can you handle our volume or format?". One or two
lines each.

### 8. SEO map
- **Target keyword cluster**: one primary and two or three secondary terms, in the ICP's own
  search language.
- **Title tag** (60 characters or fewer) and **meta description** (155 or fewer, with the metric
  and the CTA).
- **H1/H2 outline**: H1 = service + outcome; H2s = deliverables, problems, proof, process, FAQ.
- **Internal links**: homepage, sibling offer pages, the matching industry page, the matching case.

### 9. CTA
The same next step as the hero, low friction. Avoid "free pilot" language unless the company
actually runs pilots.

## Several offers
When a company has several offers (for example GIS, BIM and software development), run this mode
once per offer. Keep the shared shell identical (voice, trust bar, engagement models) and swap the
offer-specific canvas cells and matched proof, so each page is distinct and ranks for its own
cluster. If the company has a design system with per-offer colours, follow it.
