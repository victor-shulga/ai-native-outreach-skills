# Clarity scorecard: the five questions

Used in Step 4 of the rewrite. Score the CURRENT homepage, as scraped or pasted, before writing
anything new.

## A. The five questions (50 points, 10 each)

Can a visitor from the target market answer each one easily?

1. **Which services?** Is the offer readable in five seconds, or buried under a list of industries?
2. **How does it run?** Is the engagement or process shown, or only implied?
3. **What gets delivered?** Are concrete deliverables named, or vague "solutions"?
4. **When would I hire them?** Is the trigger situation clear?
5. **How does it help me progress?** Tied to the champion's work, or generic "efficiency"?

Score each 0-10: 0 = missing or misleading, 5 = present but vague, 10 = specific and written to
the champion (for example a head of engineering, a marketing lead, a CFO, whoever feels the pain
first).

## The 5-second test

Read only the first screen. Write down three things: what is sold, to whom, and the result. Any
blank means the hero fails, whatever the score above says.

## Gap format

Every gap, in the diagnosis and in the three costliest gaps:

```
<what is wrong> → <what it costs in leads> → <the fix>
```

Order gaps by cost, most costly first.

## Reading the clarity score

- **40-50**: clear. Keep the hero, spend the effort on the trust track.
- **25-39**: the visitor works to understand the offer. Rewrite the hero and the Solution block.
- **Under 25**: the copy is fighting the positioning. Rewrite the canvas first, then every block.
