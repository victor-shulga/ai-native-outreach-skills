# Persona Builder

Build a detailed buyer persona for a specific role — what they care about, how they think, what they fear, and how to write to them.

## What you need

- Role title(s) to profile
- ICP context (company type, size, industry)
- Any available data: call recordings, LinkedIn profiles, job descriptions for this role

## Process

1. Research the role using LinkedIn via MCP: look at 5–10 profiles matching the ICP for this title
2. Read their posts and activity for language patterns
3. Review job descriptions for this role to understand what success looks like for them
4. Identify:
   - Primary KPI they're measured on
   - Biggest daily frustration
   - What they're trying to prove to leadership
   - What makes them look bad internally
   - What vendors/tools they already use
   - How they prefer to buy (self-serve research vs. demo vs. committee)
5. Translate all of the above into copy guidance: tone, vocabulary, what to lead with, what to avoid

## Terms and plain-language output

<!-- glossary:start terms=persona,primary-kpi,power-phrases,decision-maker,hook -->
Rules for every document a client or their team will read (review, roast, canvas, offer cards, instruction):

1. **Open with «Словник цього документа»**: only the terms that actually appear in it, at most 10, each as «термін: що це простими словами». Like the definitions section of a contract.
2. **No internal codes or letter scales in the text** (A/B/C, D L T E, BLEND numbers without words, Own/Shared/Generic, Z1.4, GATE 2.5, T1–T6). Write the meaning instead: «є цифра з клієнтом», not «A».
3. **Use the plain form from the table below.** English stays only for tool names and for copy that ships in English (promises, hooks, subject lines). If the document is in another language, translate the plain form, never the internal term.
4. **Do not score what cannot exist yet.** A block that depends on work not done (signal catalog, case studies, a previous step) gets «пропущено: спершу потрібно <що саме>», not a score.
5. **Every section of a review opens with one concrete action** («до наступного дзвінка: …»), then at most five lines of why. No paragraph that only restates the framework.


| Internal term (this skill) | Write for the reader | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| persona, persona document | портрет покупця | опис типової людини, що купує: посада, задачі, болі, заперечення, де її шукати; будуємо лише з реальних даних. |
| Primary KPI | головний показник посади | Цифра, за якою цю людину оцінює керівництво; з неї варто починати розмову. |
| power phrases / red flag phrases | слова, що чіпляють · слова, після яких перестають читати | Мова самої посади, яку беремо в лист, і шаблонні фрази продавців, яких уникаємо. |
| decision maker | ОПР (особа, що приймає рішення) | той, хто підписує: власник, операційний директор, керівник напряму |
| hook, cold hook | гачок | перший рядок листа, через який людина читає далі |

## Output format

```
Persona: [Title]
ICP context: [Company type / size]

ROLE PROFILE
Primary KPI: [what they're measured on]
Biggest pain: [the thing that keeps them up]
What they want to prove: [internal motivation]
What makes them look bad: [risk/fear driver]
Current tools: [typical stack for this role]
Buying style: [how they prefer to evaluate]

MESSAGING GUIDE
Lead with: [the angle that resonates first]
Avoid: [what sounds like every other vendor]
Tone: [direct and blunt / peer-to-peer / analytical / etc.]
Power phrases: [words/phrases this persona responds to]
Red flag phrases: [words that make them tune out]

EXAMPLE OPENER
"[A first line that would immediately resonate with this persona]"
```

## Notes

- Build one persona file per role — reuse across all sequence-writer calls
- Language should come from the persona's own vocabulary, not your product vocabulary
- "What makes them look bad" is the most important field — fear of failure drives more decisions than hope of gain
- VP Sales and Head of Sales sound similar but often have completely different concerns — profile them separately
